How TRACEFUSE works

  1. Preserve

    Each file is copied into the case, hashed with SHA-256 and recorded in a hash-chained custody log before anything is parsed.

  2. Normalize

    Per-source time zone and clock offset are applied. Assumptions such as an inferred year are flagged, never hidden.

  3. Trace

    Every event and finding points to its source file and line, and the original line is re-verified against its hash when you open it.

TRACEFUSE runs on 127.0.0.1 by default. Don't expose it to an untrusted network.Demo data is synthetic and labelled as such.
